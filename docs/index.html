A personal fantasy football workflow

Your leagues.
Your sheets.
Your analysis.

Yahoo Fantasy Sync reads your Yahoo Fantasy Football data and organizes it in private Google Sheets, ready for your weekly analysis.

A personal, non-commercial application that runs locally on your computer. You choose the leagues, authorize access, and control the resulting spreadsheets.

Built for a weekly check-in

A clear view of each league.

01 / Context

Keep the rules with the data

Each league has its own scoring settings, roster requirements, and full player and team identifiers. One league’s rules are never assumed to apply to another.

02 / Organization

Make analysis easier

Dedicated tabs organize your roster, standings, all rosters, free agents, waivers, matchups, transactions, settings, and history.

03 / Control

Leave the decisions to you

Yahoo access is read-only: the app does not change lineups, submit waiver claims, or make trades. It writes the analysis output to Google Sheets.

An explicit connection

Your account. Your authorization.

The application uses Google OAuth to create, read, and update spreadsheets. Its Google permission covers all spreadsheets in the account, while the implementation works with the dedicated spreadsheets you configure or create through the app.

Credentials and refresh tokens stay in local files. The application does not change spreadsheet sharing permissions, sell data, or automatically send it to an AI service. This public website contains only information about the project.
